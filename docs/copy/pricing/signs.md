## Awnings

Awnings are used as the main front sign of stores. Installed on top of the entrance, they usually take the whole width of the store. Awnings are used when the design and shape of the sign itself is as important as the message. Not more than 3/4 of the front area can be used for the message.

Awnings are made in different shapes.

**All awnings include:**

- **Skeleton:** Metal structure which gives the shape of the sign. Contains lamps and all electrical equipment.
- **Skin:** A vinyl material which covers the structure.
- **Lettering:** The message is washed-out on the skin. Some vinyl lettering may be applied.

## Neon Signs

Neon signs are the most attractive signs when illuminated (day and night).

**Neon signs are composed of 3 parts:**

1. Glass tubing
2. High voltage transformer
3. A carrier board on which all tubing is installed

**Types of neon sign applications:**

- **Main front sign** - whole sign made from neon tubing without any coverage
- **Complimentary of front sign** - neon tubing added to create light and make sign more eye-catching
- **Borders only (outdoor/indoor)** - borders give impression of a larger sign or make the building stand out; mostly used around windows
- **Independent window sign** - shapes, texts, cliparts, and symbols in neon. Made from neon tubing, plastic box background, and a transformer. Ready to plug in and easy to hang. Open neon signs available in stock.

## Window Lettering

Window lettering consists of cutting vinyl logos and text messages, then applying them on glass windows of commercial units.

- White and yellow colors have the best visibility
- To use other colors, it's better to create a white or yellow outline around the letters
- Applications possible from both inside and outside
