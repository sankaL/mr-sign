## T-Shirts & Caps

Three techniques are used for printing on T-shirts and Caps:

1. **Heat Transfer (Computer Cut Material)** - Imprint cut on special Heat Seal Material, heat applied to most fabrics. Colors very sharp, even on dark fabrics.
2. **Heat Transfer (Inkjet)** - Image printed on coated paper by inkjet printer, then heat pressed. Colors not as sharp, but very fast and economical for colorful images.
3. **Silk Screening** - Most efficient and economic for large quantities. Films and screens made per color; special ink pressed onto fabric. Colors are sharp and very durable.
